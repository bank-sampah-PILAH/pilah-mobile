# Antislop UI audit 001 - 2026-10-31

Mode: AFTER (session override)

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed the legal information entry on the Pengurus web profile and SuperAdmin menu, and on the native Nasabah profile. Checked the shared legal sheet on desktop web, phone-sized web, and Android using synthetic accounts. The browser phone capture is 390 x 844. Full Flutter analysis and test suite had passed before this audit.

## Findings

1. **HIGH - R-03 Mobile Responsiveness**: On the 390 x 844 web profile, the account name and email truncate to `P...` and `pen...`, while the `Pengurus` role badge wraps into a narrow vertical stack because the logout button shares the row. Evidence: `artifacts/pr-192/web-profile-phone.png`. Source: `lib/features/profile/presentation/pages/profile_page.dart`, `_buildAccountCard`.

2. **HIGH - R-26 Interactive Elements**: The `Syarat & Ketentuan` row is disabled and has no destination. `Belum tersedia` signals the gap, but R-26 requires a real destination or removal; a placeholder also needs a `// TODO` in code and a clear visible label. No approved Terms URL was found. Evidence: `artifacts/pr-192/web-legal-phone.png` and `artifacts/pr-192/android-nasabah-legal.png`. Source: `lib/features/profile/presentation/widgets/legal_information.dart`.

## Delivery limits

The supplied Google Sites privacy policy does not define the public account-deletion procedure or confirm the information to email, ownership verification, data deleted or retained, retention periods, or handling time. The app's email link is a useful contact route, but this work cannot be described as a complete Google Play account-deletion disclosure. PIL-452 should remain In Progress until those operational details and a public deletion page are approved. No Terms destination was approved during this review.

Native Android screenshots were captured from a local debug build and include Flutter's debug ribbon. Web screenshots use synthetic accounts and local mock responses; Google sign-in against localhost was not verified.
